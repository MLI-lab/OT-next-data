For repeated verifier timeout failures, first diagnose logs and timings and fix hangs, unnecessary work, slow dependency installation, or preparation placement where possible. Choose a finite limit justified by measured timings and reasonable headroom, preserve test commands and reward semantics, and report the old and new values, affected tasks, evidence paths, and why other repairs were insufficient.

Setup review requires mean verifier preparation <= 5% of the verifier timeout and every preparation run <= 60 seconds. Increasing the verifier timeout changes the 5% allowance but never removes the 60-second cap. Do not inflate it merely to hide an unexplained setup regression. An increase for unavoidable preparation requires repeated measurements and an explicit explanation of why moving work into images or another repair was not appropriate.

For every changed task, add the change label verifier-timeout-adjusted and an evidence-backed reason through the existing patch reporting inputs (change_labels and change_reasons), so the automatic PR shows the adjustment and its justification. Mark requires_review_setup=true for preparation-related changes. Report the adjustment to the controller for stage-3 regression testing and repeated stages 4 and 5 validation.
