Exploration: Discovering an unmapped workspace before choosing an approach.
Decomposition: Explicitly listing subgoals before irreversible execution; vague intentions are insufficient.
Error Recovery: Diagnosing reported failures and adapting, rather than blindly repeating.
Specification Adherence: Rechecking the original contract late to catch output drift.
Working Memory: Preserving at least five interdependent facts across 10+ turns.
Long-Horizon Planning: Maintaining dependent steps beyond ten meaningful turns despite consequential early choices.
Constraint Satisfaction: Reconciling at least two competing hard requirements.
Modality Translation: Transferring meaning between modalities, such as diagrams and code.
Reverse Engineering: Inferring concealed structure or purpose from opaque artifacts.
Mathematical Derivation: Establishing a formula or proof before implementation.
Adversarial Reasoning: Seeking ways around intended controls or exploitable weaknesses.
